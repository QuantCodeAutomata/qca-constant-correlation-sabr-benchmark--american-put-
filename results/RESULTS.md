## Configuration Notes

Experiments were run with reduced sample sizes for practical runtime:
- SABR (Exp 1): M=100000, N_T∈{10,50,100} (paper: {100,200,400}), 3 seeds
- SABSR (Exp 2): M=100000, N_T=100 (paper: 400), 3 seeds
- HSLV (Exp 3): M=200000, N_T∈{10,20,30} (paper values), 3 seeds
- HSCLV (Exp 4): M=200000, N_T=30 (paper value), 3 seeds
- Evolution (Exp 5): M=50000, SABSR N_T=50, HSCLV N_T=30, 1 seed

All models and methodology follow Yang & Li (2024a) exactly.
XGBoost params: max_depth=2, lr=0.9, n_estimators=150, early_stopping_rounds=1, 75/25 train-test split.
